Global Tariff Database

The Global Tariff Database (GTD) provides bilateral statutory tariff rates for 200 importers and their partners, spanning 34 years (1988–2021). It includes both MFN and preferential tariffs and is based on HS6-level tariff rates (HS88/92 Nomenclature), covering over 6.9 billion observations.

The GTD does not include tariff types beyond MFN and preferential tariffs, such as safeguards, anti-dumping tariffs, or those related to the US trade war.

Find details about the available variables in the GTD and the reference data in the data dictionary. The accompanying paper and its Online Appendix C provide a detailed description of the algorithm.

Global Tariff Database: U.S. Trade War Extension

The Global Tariff Database: U.S. Trade War Extension (GTD—U.S. Trade War) provides daily, product-level data on U.S. import and export tariffs vis-à-vis all trading partners from January 2018 through August 15, 2025. This extension builds on the Global Tariff Database (v beta1-2024-12), incorporating all tariff measures imposed by the United States and applied to U.S. exports abroad during the 2018–2025 trade wars, consolidated into a single dataset. For an extensive description of the construction of the GTD—U.S. Trade War, please refer to the accompanying paper and its Online Appendix A. The data will be updated periodically to include new tariff measures as they are announced and implemented, although typically with some lag.

Military Goods

The list contains the 44 HS6 product codes (HS 2017 nomenclature) of the European Union's Common High Priority (CHP) items: dual-use goods such as semiconductors, ball bearings and communication equipment that are critical for Russian weapon systems and have repeatedly been found in Russian military equipment in Ukraine. The list is used in Scheckenhofer et al. (2025).

EU Sanctions on Russia 2014

The list contains all 478 CN8 product codes (Combined Nomenclature 2014) covered by the trade restrictions the European Union imposed on Russia in 2014: the arms embargo (Council Decision 2014/512/CFSP), which bans both exports to and imports from Russia, and the export authorization requirement for oil-sector equipment (Council Regulation (EU) No 833/2014). The product codes are taken from the EU's TARIC database. The list is used in Flach et al. (2024).

Russia's 2014 counter-sanctions (the import ban on agricultural products and foodstuffs) are not included; see Crozet and Hinz (2020) for the list of affected products.
